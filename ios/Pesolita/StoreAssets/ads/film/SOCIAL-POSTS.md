## LinkedIn (hero H1 or the cut-down)

**Post:**
Five days before payday. That's when most budgets break. 💸

In the Philippines we have a name for it: **petsa de peligro**, the "danger days" before sahod. It isn't about overspending on one big thing. It's the small leaks: ₱180 milk tea, ₱350 rides, a ₱1,299 cart, a generous "libre ko na."

So I made a movie-trailer parody about it for **Pesolita**, the budget app I'm building for Filipinos. 🎬

What Pesolita does differently:
→ **Where it went:** spending by category, so the leaks are obvious
→ **Safe to spend today:** a daily number that makes your money last until payday
→ **4-second logging:** fast enough that you actually keep doing it
→ **One wallet:** cash, debit, credit and e-wallets together

How the ad was made: AI-generated scenes (Google Flow) for the story, real app footage for the product, and kinetic typography and original sound design, all edited in code with Remotion.

Personal finance tools don't fail on features. They fail on habit. The goal is to make checking your money feel as easy as checking your messages.

What's the one spending "leak" you noticed too late this month?
